PhD candidate · ENS de Lyon

I am interested in turbulence in a broad sense: how physical systems develop a multiscale behavior? What are the consequences of such multiscale behavior (at PDE level)? How can we model such systems? I am advised by Laurent Chevillard and Nikolay Tzvetkov.

CV Google Scholar

Research interests

01

Roughness and PDEs

Ill-posedness and probabilistic well-posedness for nonlinear nonlinear wave equations.

02

Spontaneous stochasticity

Giving a well defined probabilistic meaning to inviscid limits such as the paradigmatic Navier-Stokes to Euler limit.

03

Statistical modeling of turbulent systems

Stochastic models and toy models encoding key features of turbulent flows
